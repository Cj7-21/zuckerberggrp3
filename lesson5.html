📖 Lesson 5 – Online Safety

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the Internet as an information superhighway.
  • Identify possible online risks and misuse of ICT.
  • Recognize different Internet threats.
  • Apply practical tips to stay safe online.

Internet as the Information Superhighway

The Internet is called an information superhighway because anyone who has access can place information and grab that information quickly and widely.

Possible Risks Online

  • Exposure to inappropriate content
  • Lifestyle websites such as self‑harm and hate sites
  • Privacy issues
  • Health and well‑being concerns
  • Prolonged online exposure
  • Addiction to gambling and gaming
  • Theft and fraud
  • Social pressure
  • Viruses

Minor Misuse of ICT

  • Plagiarism and copyright infringement
  • Downloading irrelevant materials
  • Misconduct in logins
  • Using of phone during class
  • Unauthorized taking of pictures

Tips to Stay Safe Online

  1. Be Mindful: Be careful of what you share online.
  2. Read Terms and Conditions: Do not just accept them without reading.
  3. Check Privacy Policy Page: Learn how the website handles your information.
  4. Use Security Features: Turn on the security features of your social networking sites.
  5. Do Not Share Password: Never share your password with anyone.
  6. Avoid Public Wi‑Fi for Sensitive Logins: Incognito or private mode will not fully protect you.
  7. Do Not Talk to Strangers: Whether face‑to‑face or online.
  8. Never Post Vacation Plans Online: This can alert others that your home is empty.
  9. Add Friends You Know in Real Life: Avoid accepting unknown friend requests.
  10. Avoid Visiting Untrusted Websites: Do not click suspicious links.

Internet Threats

Threat Description
Malware Stands for malicious software.
Virus A malicious program designed to replicate itself and transfer from one computer to another.
Worm A malicious program that spreads itself from one computer to another using networks.
Trojan A malicious program disguised as useful software; once installed, it leaves your PC unprotected for hackers.
Rogue Security Software Tricks users into believing it is real security software.
Spyware Runs in the background without your knowledge and monitors your activities.
Keyloggers Records keystrokes to steal passwords and sensitive information.
Adware Sends advertisements mostly as pop‑ups.
Spam Unwanted email mostly from bots or advertisers.
Phishing Aims to acquire sensitive personal information such as passwords and credit card details.
Pharming A more complicated form of phishing that exploits the DNS system.

Example: ILOVEYOU Virus

The ILOVEYOU worm/virus was created by Filipinos. It spread via email with the subject line "ILOVEYOU" and an attachment. In just 10 days, it affected an estimated 45 million users and caused about 10 billion damages.
