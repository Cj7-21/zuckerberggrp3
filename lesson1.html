📖 Lesson 1: Introduction to ICT

Learning Objectives

  • Define what ICT stands for and what it means.
  • Identify and explain the five elements of an ICT system.
  • Understand the difference between data and information.
  • Recognize the importance of ICT in daily life.
  • Identify famous pioneers of computing and common shortcut keys.

What is ICT?

ICT = Information and Communications Technology

ICT refers to all technologies used to create, store, exchange, and use information. It combines computing technology with communication technology.

Simple Definition: ICT is the use of digital tools to find, understand, create, and share information.

The Five Elements of an ICT System

Element Description Examples
Peopleware The people who use and develop technology Users, programmers, technicians
Hardware Physical parts you can touch Monitor, keyboard, CPU, phone
Software Programs that run on hardware Windows, browsers, MS Office
Data Raw facts and figures Numbers, text, images
Procedures Rules and steps to follow User manuals, safety guidelines

Data vs. Information

Data: Raw, unprocessed facts (e.g., numbers, names, dates).

Information: Processed, organized data that has meaning.

Example: "85" = Data → "Your score is 85" = Information.
