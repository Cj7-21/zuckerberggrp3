Lesson 1 – Introduction to ICT (Identification – 10 items)

Instructions: Identify what is being described.

  1. It stands for Information and Communications Technology.
  2. It refers to the physical parts of a computer.
  3. It refers to programs and applications that run on computers.
  4. It is used to enter data into the computer.
  5. It displays information from the computer.
  6. It stores files permanently inside the computer.
  7. It is the brain of the computer.
  8. It is used to print documents.
  9. It is temporary memory used by the computer.
  10. It is the operating system that manages computer resources.

Lesson 2 – Internet & WWW (Multiple‑Choice – 10 items)

Instructions: Choose the correct answer A, B, or C.

  1. What is the Internet?
    • A. Global network of computers
    • B. One website
    • C. A mobile app
  2. What is WWW?
    • A. World Wide Web
    • B. World Wide Wallet
    • C. Wide Web Water
  3. Which is an ICT trend?
    • A. Cloud Computing
    • B. Notebook only
    • C. Pen
  4. IoT means?
    • A. Internet of Things
    • B. Internet of Time
    • C. Input of Things
  5. Mobile Technology refers to?
    • A. Smartphones and portable devices
    • B. Desk only
    • C. Books
  6. Social Media is used for?
    • A. Online interaction
    • B. Locking doors only
    • C. Printing only
  7. E‑Learning means?
    • A. Learning online
    • B. Eating lessons
    • C. Writing only
  8. Cloud Computing stores data?
    • A. Online servers
    • B. Only inside one computer
    • C. Paper
  9. Artificial Intelligence means?
    • A. Machines simulating human intelligence
    • B. Only typing
    • C. Drawing only
  10. Internet uses?
    • A. Networks to connect computers
    • B. Pencils only
    • C. Chairs only

Lesson 4 – Netiquette (Paragraph Writing – 10 sentence activity)

Instructions: Write a paragraph with at least 10 sentences explaining why netiquette is important for students. Include rules such as respect, proper language, privacy, honesty, and responsible posting.

Lesson 5 – Online Safety (True or False – 10 items)

Instructions: Write True if correct and False if wrong.

  1. Sharing your password freely is safe.
  2. Clicking unknown links may cause phishing.
  3. Updating passwords regularly helps protect accounts.
  4. Posting full address online is safe.
  5. Using antivirus helps protect devices.
  6. Replying to suspicious emails is safe.
  7. Not sharing OTP to others is good practice.
  8. Cyberbullying is acceptable online.
  9. Backing‑up files protects your data.
  10. Checking website URLs before entering data is important.
