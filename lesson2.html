📖 Lesson 2: Internet, World Wide Web & ICT Trends

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish between the Internet and the World Wide Web.
  • Identify the functions and components of ICT related to the Internet.
  • Describe the different ICT trends.
  • Identify different types of websites.

ICT: Information and Communications Technology

ICT refers to technologies used to:

  • Create information
  • Process information
  • Store information
  • Retrieve information
  • Share information
  • Communicate information

Components of ICT

Component Description
Hardware Physical devices such as computers and smartphones
Software Programs that instruct hardware
Internet A worldwide network enabling communication and information sharing

Internet vs. World Wide Web

Internet World Wide Web (WWW)
The infrastructure — a massive global network connecting billions of devices. The content — pages, documents, and websites accessed through the Internet.
Like the road system. Like the buildings and shops on the roads.

Remember: The Web is on top of the Internet — they are NOT the same thing!

ICT Trends

Trend Description
Artificial Intelligence (AI) Technology that enables machines to simulate human intelligence
Cloud Computing Storing and accessing data and programs over the Internet
Internet of Things (IoT) Physical objects connected to the Internet to exchange data
Social Media Online platforms for connecting and sharing content
Mobile Technology Portable technology enabling anytime‑anywhere access
E‑Learning Learning delivered through digital platforms

Common Types of Websites

Type Description Examples
Blog Personal articles, opinions, journal entries News blogs, personal journals
Social Media Used to connect and share with others Facebook, Instagram
E‑Commerce Buying and selling goods online Shopee, Lazada
Educational Provides learning resources Sites with .edu domain
Government Provides public services and information Sites with .gov domain
Wiki Collaborative information that anyone can edit Wikipedia
